How to use these supply hubs

iComply Supplies is the catalogue on this website. It is not a second shop with its own checkout. Each card takes the live title, SKU and GBP price from the Shopify product dump, and the order button opens that product on shop.icomplypropertyservices.co.uk. If a price is missing, ask for a written quote rather than guessing from a similar part. Call 07517 806082 or use WhatsApp under the heading and send the code you are looking at.

The four hubs are Fire, Electrical, Security and Gas. Fire is the large set: panels, detectors, call points, sounders, AOV equipment and wireless devices that were in the dump. Electrical is only the products that are actually electrical supplies, such as batteries, fire cable, clips and refuge equipment. Security is the CAME, Videx and Bell System products in the dump. Gas has its own page because the dump did not contain a clearly gas SKU, and that page says so.

Use a hub when you already know the trade. Use the search on Shopify when you have a manufacturer code. A landlord who needs an EICR, a gas safety record or a fire risk assessment should use the service pages, not a product card. Supply pages sell parts. Service pages book the visit.

What a product card is promising

The card shows the name Shopify published, the vendor, the product type and the price range of the variants. It does not add a discount, a pack quantity or a lead time that the dump did not contain. Photos are the Shopify images. If a product has no image, the card says the image is coming, and you can still order or enquire.

Trade buyers can send the SKU, the quantity and the delivery postcode. Homeowners can send a photo of the failed part and the address. We will say whether the part on the card is the right one before you pay. The office is 17 Woodlands Park Road, Offerton, Stockport SK2 5DE. Collection is only arranged when we confirm it for that SKU.

Fire supplies

Open the fire hub when the part is a panel, a detector, a call point, a sounder, a beacon, an interface, a wireless device or an AOV accessory that was in the dump. Advanced, Apollo, C-TEC, Haes, Hochiki, Kentec, KAC and Nittan codes sit here when the title matched the fire rules. A code that is really a cable or a battery is on the electrical hub instead, even if an engineer fits it next to a panel.

Do not treat a fire card as a design. A listed detector does not mean the loop, the cause-and-effect or the zone chart is already agreed. If the building needs a new system or a takeover, use the fire alarm service page and ask for a survey. The catalogue is for the part once the model is known. The survey is for the building when the model is not known.

Electrical supplies

The electrical hub is a short list on purpose. It holds the batteries, fire cable, clips and refuge items that the classifier kept out of the fire grid. Prysmian cable codes belong there. If a cable code is missing, send the drum size and the colour rather than ordering a nearby code that has a different core count. We will not relabel a fire detector as electrical just to make this hub look larger.

An EICR, a new circuit or an EV charger is a visit. Those pages are under electrical services. Buying a battery from this hub does not book the test, and booking the test does not put a battery in the basket. Say which one you need when you call.

Security supplies

The security hub lists CAME, Videx and Bell System products that were in the dump: barrier parts, door-entry modules and the related trade packs. A car park barrier that needs a survey, a loop or a safety edge is a barriers visit, not a card. Use the barriers service page for the installation and this hub only when you already have the part code.

Intercom stations and handsets follow the same rule. If the photograph of the failed station matches a listed module, order that module. If the system is a different brand, or the station is unmarked, send the photo before you pay. We would rather say the code is not in the dump than ship the wrong entrance panel.

Gas supplies

The gas hub explains why it has no product cards. The dump did not contain a SKU that was clearly a gas part, so the page stays empty of products. Gas work is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. A landlord gas safety record, a boiler service or a repair is booked from the gas service page. If you smell gas, leave the property and call the gas emergency service before you message us about a part.

Prices, delivery and a wrong part

A price on a card is the Shopify price at the time the hub was built. It can change on the shop host before you check out. The button on the card is the place to confirm the current price. We do not keep a second price list on this page. Delivery is whatever that product offers at checkout. If you need the part on a particular day, say so before you pay, because the dump does not include a promised lead time.

If the part arrives and it is the wrong code, do not fit it. Photograph the label and the part you removed, and contact us with the order reference. We will tell you whether it goes back to the shop host or whether an engineer should identify the replacement on site. Fitting the wrong detector or the wrong module to make a panel go quiet is not a repair we will describe as complete.

Questions about the catalogue

Why are there two shop addresses?

This site lists the products. Checkout stays on shop.icomplypropertyservices.co.uk. The address without a trailing slash redirects here, so there is one catalogue URL.

Can I collect from Stockport?

Ask when you enquire. The office is 17 Woodlands Park Road, Offerton, Stockport SK2 5DE. Delivery is whatever Shopify offers for that SKU.

What if the part I need is not listed?

Send the code or a photo. We will say if we can supply it or if an engineer needs to see the job. We do not invent a product page for a code that is not in the dump.

Does a card include installation?

No. The card is the part. Installation, commissioning and certification are booked on the matching service page, with a written quote before work starts.
