Why this gas page has no products

The public Shopify product dump did not contain a product that is clearly a gas part. This page stays empty of SKUs on purpose. An empty list is more useful than a boiler flange or a flue copied in from another trade. When a real gas SKU is published on the store, it will be classified onto this hub and the count above will change. Until that happens, the honest page is this one: no cards, a written quote, and a clear route to the visit.

If you need a landlord gas safety record, a boiler service or a repair, that is a visit, not a basket. Gas work is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. Call 07517 806082, send the address and say whether the property is occupied. The survey and the written quote come before any work is agreed. The office is 17 Woodlands Park Road, Offerton, Stockport SK2 5DE.

What to send with a gas enquiry

Send the postcode, the appliance make and model if you can read it, and a photo of the data plate or the fault. Say whether you smell gas. If you smell gas, leave the property, avoid switches and call the gas emergency service first. We will not treat a smell of gas as a parts order. After the emergency service has made the property safe, we can talk about the repair and the written quote.

Landlords should say how many appliances are on the tenancy and whether the record is a renewal. Homeowners should say whether anyone is living in the house and whether the heating is off. We quote the visit in writing. We do not put a parts rate card on this empty catalogue page, and we do not invent a price for a flue, a valve or a heat exchanger that is not in the dump.

What this page will not pretend to sell

A combi boiler, a system boiler, a cooker, a fire and a meter are not listed here, because they were not in the dump as gas products. Naming them on an empty page would look like stock. It is not stock. If an engineer needs one of those appliances for a job, the quote names the appliance after the survey, and the supply route is agreed then. This hub does not take a card payment for an appliance it cannot show.

Detectors, valves and accessories sometimes appear in the fire or plumbing titles of the dump. If a code is clearly one of those, it stays on the hub that matched the title. We will point you at that card. We will not copy the same SKU onto this page to make the gas count look higher. One product, one hub.

Landlord records and homeowner repairs

A landlord gas safety record is a check of the appliances and the flue, with a written record afterwards. It is not a bag of parts. Book it from the gas service page or the landlord pack if you want the record alongside the other landlord certificates. Tell us the renewal date and whether the tenants will be in. We will say if the date is free.

A homeowner with no heating should say how long it has been off and whether there is hot water from another source. A no-heating call is still a visit. We do not ask you to buy a part from this empty page before someone has seen the appliance. If the fault is a known part and that part later appears as a SKU, we will send the link. If it does not, the quote includes the part the engineer specifies.

Where the other supplies are

Fire, electrical and security products that were in the dump are on their own hubs. A gas engineer sometimes needs a detector or a valve that is sold as a fire or plumbing accessory. If that accessory has a Shopify SKU, it is listed on the hub that matched its title, not invented here. Browse those hubs, or ask us to check the code. Electrical cable and batteries are on the electrical hub. Barrier and intercom parts are on the security hub. None of those cards are gas appliances.

Questions about gas supplies

Will you add a boiler just to fill this page?

No. A product appears here only when the Shopify dump contains it and it is clearly gas. Until then the page explains how to book the work.

Who does the gas work?

Gas work is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. iComply arranges the visit from Stockport and sends the written quote first.

Can I order a flue or a valve?

Send the manufacturer code. If it is a live SKU we will point you at the product. If it is not in the dump we will say so and quote the visit if a part has to be identified on site.

What if I smell gas?

Leave the property, avoid switches and call the gas emergency service. Contact us about the repair only after the property is safe.
